An analysis of the `pitch_search_3tap` function and its usage within the speex codebase reveals that it is not called directly, but rather through a function pointer, `ltp_quant`, in the `nb_encode` function. The requirements for `pitch_search_3tap` are derived from how its parameters are initialized in `nb_encode`.

The function `pitch_search_3tap` performs a pitch search and quantization for the Speex codec. It finds the best pitch and gain from a codebook and writes the quantized indices to the bitstream. It also updates the excitation and target signals. The function is highly dependent on the encoder state and various parameters from the Speex mode and submode configurations.
