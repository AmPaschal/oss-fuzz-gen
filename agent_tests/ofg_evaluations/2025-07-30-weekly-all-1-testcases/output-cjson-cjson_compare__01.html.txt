
	
<description>
The cJSON_Compare function compares two cJSON objects for equality. It recursively traverses the objects, comparing them based on their type and value. For objects and arrays, it iterates through their children and compares them one by one. The case_sensitive flag is used when comparing the keys of object members.
</description>
